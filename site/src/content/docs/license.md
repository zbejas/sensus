Sensus is released under the **Apache License 2.0**: the whole project, with no open-core
split, no gated features, and no license keys. The full text is in the repository's
[LICENSE](https://github.com/zbejas/sensus/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the same license, with **no CLA**: what comes in is
licensed exactly like what goes out. Contributors are asked to sign off their commits; the
details are in the
[contributor guide](https://github.com/zbejas/sensus/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Attribution

The [NOTICE](https://github.com/zbejas/sensus/blob/main/NOTICE) file records the copyright
line and the credit to OpenCode (MIT), whose harness and TUI work inspired parts of sensus.

## Third-party code

Bundled dependencies are limited to permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause,
BSD-3-Clause, or ISC), and a build fails if a bundled package is missing a license or uses
one outside that list.
